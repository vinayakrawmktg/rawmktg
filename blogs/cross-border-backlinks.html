Beyond Cross-Border: Where India's Global-Payments Brands Actually Earn Their Backlinks

A neutral, data-led look at the topics that win links for Cashfree, PayGlocal, Skydo, Infinity, Eximpay and Xflow.

Based on an Ahrefs link-intersect snapshot covering ~9,515 referring pages from ~1,788 unique domains across six India cross-border payments companies, June 2026.

India has quietly become one of the most competitive markets in the world for cross-border payments. A cluster of companies now competes to help Indian exporters, freelancers, startups and platforms move money across borders, and that competition is playing out not only in product and pricing, but in content and search visibility. To understand how these brands build authority online, we analysed a snapshot of their backlink profiles and classified every referring page by topic.

The dataset is an Ahrefs link-intersect export captured in June 2026, covering roughly 9,515 referring pages from about 1,788 unique domains that point to one or more of six players: Cashfree, PayGlocal, Skydo, Infinity, Eximpay and Xflow. We grouped each link into one of around fifteen topics to see which subjects the category uses to earn coverage. The headline takeaway is simple: the brands that win most do not rely on "cross-border payments" content alone.

Why does this matter? In a market where the underlying service, moving money in and out of India, is broadly similar across providers, search visibility and editorial coverage become real points of differentiation. The brands that appear across a wide range of relevant publications build trust with both readers and search engines, and they capture demand from buyers who may never type the phrase "cross-border payments" at all. Reading the link data in aggregate, rather than brand by brand, shows what the category collectively treats as link-worthy.

01The Category at a Glance

Backlink profiles in this group vary enormously in size, and size tracks business scope rather than success at cross-border payments specifically. Cashfree sits far ahead on raw volume. The pure-play specialists run smaller, more focused profiles.

02Links Spread Across Far More Than One Topic

The most striking pattern is how wide the category casts its net. Across all six brands, genuine topical backlinks fall into roughly fourteen active subject areas. Cross-border payments is only one of many, and by unique referring domains it is not even the largest.

Payment-gateway and fintech content, software and developer pages, e-commerce and online-selling resources, fintech news and PR, business and SME advice, and startup coverage all rival or exceed cross-border content as sources of links. In other words, these companies earn authority by showing up in the wider financial-operations conversation, not just in articles with "cross-border" in the title.

Fig. 2 · Topics ranked by unique referring domains across all six brands (self-referral, spam and off-topic excluded)

The breadth of the topic map reflects the breadth of the cross-border customer base. Software and developer content earns links because the API layer is often where integrations are researched. Payment-gateway and fintech coverage earns links because cross-border rails sit alongside domestic rails in most industry roundups. E-commerce links come from marketplace sellers collecting internationally. News and PR links come from the milestone-driven coverage cycle that governs the fintech press.

Cross-border remittance as a standalone topic sits mid-table, meaning it is clearly relevant but not sufficient on its own to build a competitive profile. A brand that publishes only about cross-border payments is optimising for a fraction of the available link surface.

03Scale vs Focus: Two Different Playbooks

The data reveals two distinct approaches. Cashfree's broad, high-volume footprint reflects a full-stack payments business. The pure-play specialists show more instructive strategies: Skydo disperses across adjacent niches, PayGlocal leans into news and PR.
Playbook A

Cashfree: full-stack breadth

1,372 unique domains, largest clusters in software/developer (296) and payment-gateway/fintech (284). Roughly 30% of pages in this dataset are self-referrals from documentation and brand subdomains; ~14% are low-quality or spam-type pages, a useful reminder that raw link counts can overstate editorial reach.

Playbook B

Specialists: deliberate adjacency

Skydo (215 domains) spreads across cross-border (35), freelancing (26), software (25), e-commerce (18) and directories (15). PayGlocal (318 domains) leans into news/PR (43), cross-border (39), SME-business (32), payment-gateway (29) and banking/personal finance (20). Two brands, two visible strategies, both well beyond the core topic.

04The Adjacent Topics That Do the Work

Looking only at the pure-play peers strips out Cashfree's scale and shows which secondary subjects realistically earn links for a cross-border specialist. A handful stand out, each closely tied to who actually sends and receives international payments.
  • Compliance, tax and regulation. Content on GST, FEMA, RBI purpose codes, FIRC and FIRA documentation, and reconciliation sits naturally beside cross-border payments, because every inbound payment carries a compliance trail. These pages attract durable, informational links from accounting and tax publications such as IBS Intelligence and TaxGuru.
  • Freelancing and the gig economy. Independent professionals invoicing overseas clients are a core cross-border audience, and freelancing blogs link readily to payment tools. This is Skydo's second-largest cluster and an obvious fit for any brand serving solo earners.
  • Exports, import and trade. Exporters and IT-services firms are central to the category, yet trade and EXIM publications remain comparatively lightly tapped, with links appearing on outlets like CXOToday and The Fintech Times.
  • E-commerce and online selling. Direct-to-consumer and marketplace sellers collecting from foreign buyers generate a large pool of links, including listings on app marketplaces and seller-focused blogs.
  • Funded startups. Companies moving money between a global parent and an Indian subsidiary draw coverage from founder and venture media such as YourStory, CB Insights and Y Combinator's directories.
  • Fintech news and PR. The highest-authority and most repeatable links in the whole dataset come from trade press, including PYMNTS, FF News, The Paypers, IBS Intelligence and Tech in Asia, earned through funding announcements and product news rather than written content.

05How These Links Are Earned

The topics above are earned through three recognisable mechanisms, and the mix differs by brand. No single tactic dominates, and breadth of topic tends to come from breadth of method.
01

Digital public relations

Funding rounds, product launches and partnership announcements that trade publications cover as news. PayGlocal's profile leans heavily this way, which is why news and PR is its single largest cluster. These links tend to be high in authority but episodic, tied to company milestones rather than steady output. PYMNTS, FF News, The Paypers and IBS Intelligence are the publications that repeatedly appear.

02

Resource and reference content

Explainer guides, calculators, and tool pages on subjects like purpose codes, currency conversion or invoicing. This content earns links slowly but compounds, because other writers cite it when they need a reliable reference. It is the engine behind the compliance, banking and freelancing clusters, and it is the most repeatable tactic for a brand willing to invest in it.

03

Listings and directories

Software marketplaces, comparison pages, "best of" round-ups and startup databases. These are the easiest links to obtain, often through a simple submission, and they explain why directories and software pages appear so prominently across every brand in the dataset. They rarely move the needle alone, but they establish baseline presence and are quick to secure.

Most of the stronger profiles combine all three. Skydo pairs steady reference content with directory presence. Cashfree layers a large developer-documentation footprint on top of news coverage. The pattern suggests that no single tactic dominates, and that breadth of topic tends to come from breadth of method.

06What This Means for the Category

Topical authority in cross-border payments is built across a constellation of related subjects, not a single keyword theme. The open ground lies in export and trade content, compliance and tax, and freelancing, all comparatively lightly tapped even among established players.

For marketers and operators in cross-border payments, the data points to a clear, neutral conclusion: the brands with the most resilient profiles treat compliance, freelancing, exports, e-commerce, startups and trade press as part of one connected story about helping businesses operate globally.

It also suggests where the open ground lies. Several genuinely relevant niches, particularly exports and trade, tax and compliance, and freelancing, show relatively few referring domains even among the specialists, which means they are far from saturated. A newer entrant such as Xflow, which currently shows a minimal footprint in this dataset, has a clearly mapped set of adjacent topics to pursue, the same ones its established peers already use.

None of this is a verdict on which brand is "winning". A larger link count can reflect a broader business rather than a sharper strategy, and a smaller, focused profile like Skydo's or PayGlocal's can be more efficient per link earned. The fairer reading is that the category has collectively mapped out which adjacent subjects pay off, and that map is now visible to anyone willing to study it.

Frequently Asked Questions

Which topics generate the most backlinks for cross-border payments companies in India?

Software and developer content, payment-gateway and fintech coverage, directories and listings, and fintech news and PR generate more unique referring domains than cross-border payments content itself. Across the six companies analyzed, cross-border remittance is one of fourteen active topic clusters and is not the largest by unique referring domains.

What is the difference between Cashfree and pure-play cross-border specialists in link strategy?

Cashfree has a broad profile driven by its full-stack payments scope: 1,372 unique referring domains, with software and developer pages its largest cluster at 296 domains. Pure-play specialists like Skydo (215 domains) and PayGlocal (318 domains) use more focused strategies. Skydo spreads across cross-border, freelancing, software, e-commerce, and directories. PayGlocal leans heavily on news and PR, its largest single cluster at 43 domains.

Which adjacent topics are underserved in India cross-border payments SEO?

Exports and trade, compliance and tax regulation (GST, FEMA, RBI purpose codes), and freelancing show relatively few referring domains even among the established specialists, indicating they are not saturated. A newer entrant like Xflow has a clearly mapped set of adjacent topics to pursue, the same ones its established peers already use.

Methodology and Caveats
  • Source: a single Ahrefs link-intersect snapshot (referring pages and subdomains) captured in June 2026, covering about 9,515 referring pages across roughly 1,788 domains.
  • Topics were assigned with keyword rules over each page's title, URL and referring domain, so figures are directional rather than exact; a minority of pages remain uncategorised.
  • Self-referral pages (a brand's own documentation and subdomains), spam or low-quality pages, and clearly off-topic pages were separated out and excluded from the topical comparisons.
  • Counts use unique referring domains where possible, since one domain can link many times; this is a fairer measure of breadth than raw page counts.
  • Backlink data reflects links discovered by Ahrefs and is not a complete census of every live link. No brand in this article sponsored or reviewed the analysis.
