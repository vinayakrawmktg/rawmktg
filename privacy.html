3. Cookies and Tracking Technologies

We use cookies and similar tracking technologies on the Site. Cookies are small text files placed on your device that help us understand how the Site is used and improve your experience.

Cookie typePurpose and examples
Essential cookiesRequired for the Site to function. These cannot be disabled. They include session management and security tokens.
Analytics cookiesMeasure how visitors use the Site (pages visited, time on site, traffic source). We use tools such as Google Analytics / GA4 for this purpose.
Marketing & advertising cookiesUsed to show relevant ads to you on other platforms and to measure ad campaign performance. This includes pixels from LinkedIn (LinkedIn Insight Tag), Meta, and similar ad platforms.
Preference cookiesRemember choices you make on the Site, such as language or display preferences.

Managing cookies

You can control and delete cookies through your browser settings. You can also opt out of specific tracking as follows:

  • Google Analytics: use the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on at tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout
  • LinkedIn Insight Tag: visit linkedin.com/psettings/guest-controls/retargeting-opt-out
  • Meta (Facebook) pixel: visit facebook.com/settings and adjust your ad preferences
  • General advertising opt-out: visit youradchoices.com or youronlinechoices.eu

Please note that disabling certain cookies may affect the functionality of the Site.

Google AdSense and third-party advertising

This Site uses Google AdSense, a third-party advertising service provided by Google, to display advertisements. In connection with serving ads, the following applies:

  • Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this Site and other websites.
  • Google's use of advertising cookies (including the DoubleClick DART cookie) enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visit to this Site and/or other sites on the Internet.
  • You may opt out of personalised advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings (adssettings.google.com).
  • Alternatively, you can opt out of some third-party vendors' use of cookies for personalised advertising by visiting aboutads.info/choices or, in the EU, youronlinechoices.eu.
  • For more information on how Google uses data when you use our partners' sites or apps, see Google's Privacy & Terms and the Google advertising policies.

If you are in the European Economic Area, United Kingdom, or another region where consent is required, ad personalisation is applied in accordance with applicable consent requirements.

9. US Privacy Rights (California Residents, CCPA/CPRA)

If you are a California resident, the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA) and California Privacy Rights Act (CPRA) give you the following rights:

  • Right to know: request disclosure of the categories and specific pieces of personal information we have collected about you in the past 12 months
  • Right to delete: request deletion of your personal information, subject to certain exceptions
  • Right to correct: request correction of inaccurate personal information
  • Right to opt out of sale or sharing: we do not sell personal information. We do use third-party advertising cookies, which may constitute "sharing" under CCPA. You can opt out via our cookie controls or by contacting us.
  • Right to non-discrimination: we will not discriminate against you for exercising your CCPA rights

To submit a California privacy request, contact us at vinayak@rawmktg.com with "CCPA Request" in the subject line. We will respond within 45 days.

We do not knowingly sell or share personal information of consumers under 16 years of age.
