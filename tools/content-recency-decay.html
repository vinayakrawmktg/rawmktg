How this works

AI engines increasingly weight freshness. Pages left unrefreshed for 90+ days are roughly 3.2x more likely to lose their AI citations, and over 80% of citations on commercial queries come from pages updated within the prior 30 days. Each engine applies a different window, so this tool models an exponential freshness half-life per engine and projects retention forward.

Use the recommended refresh date as a trigger to update one statistic, the dateModified field, and one section heading, enough to re-enter the citation window.

Frequently asked questions

How fast do AI citations decay?

It varies by engine and topic, but pages left un-updated lose citation share measurably within a quarter, faster on recency-sensitive engines like Perplexity and Google's AI surfaces. The estimator models the curve so you refresh before the drop, not after.

How often should I refresh a page?

Refresh on a cadence tied to the topic's volatility: fast-moving categories every 4-8 weeks, evergreen ones quarterly. Update the substance and the dateModified in schema, a date change alone does not reset the clock.
