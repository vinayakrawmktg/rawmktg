The formula

Engines do not perform uniformly, so pooling them into one average hides the thing you need to see. Visibility is a weighted composite: for each engine, mentions divided by prompts issued, times a normalised platform weight. The score renormalises over the engines you actually track, so skipping two engines does not silently understate you.

Visibility is your rate; Share of Model is your share of the field. Enter the total brand-mentions across every vendor in the same responses as "field mentions", and the tool divides your weighted presence by the weighted field presence. Run a fixed prompt set 8 to 12 times per engine and average before entering counts, single runs are noisy.

Frequently asked questions

Why weight visibility by platform?

Because engines do not perform uniformly, and pooling them into one average hides where you win or lose. A weighted score, normalised over the engines you actually track, reflects the engines your buyers use instead of treating all six as equal.

How many runs should I average before entering counts?

Eight to twelve per engine for a decision-grade Share of Model number (three to five is fine for a quick directional read). AI answers are non-deterministic, so a single run is a sample of size one; averaging smooths the run-to-run variance, and high live-retrieval engines like Perplexity need the top of that range.
