What you are paying for

Every plan includes the crawl diagnostic: we fetch your pages the way an AI crawler does, identify the specific technical and content faults preventing retrieval, and generate the fixes. Paid plans add measurement — we ask AI assistants real buying questions on a schedule and report how often your brand is named and recommended, relative to your competitors.

Measurement is reported with confidence intervals. Where a change is not statistically distinguishable from noise, we say so rather than drawing an arrow.
